BARNSTORM
A little pixel plane you can fly over any web page.

Two games in one: WRECK, where you shoot the page to bits while dwarf
builders try to put it back, and SORTIE, a top-down shooter against waves
of enemy aircraft. Nothing on the page is really changed: land, and it's
all as it was.


INSTALL IT (about a minute)

It works in Chrome, and in other browsers built on it (Edge, Brave, Arc).

1. Keep this folder somewhere it can stay, like Documents. The browser
   runs the game from here, so if you delete or move the folder later,
   the game goes with it.

2. In Chrome, open a new tab and go to:   chrome://extensions
   (In Edge it's edge://extensions, in Brave brave://extensions.)

3. Turn on "Developer mode". It's a switch at the top right.

4. Click "Load unpacked" and choose THIS folder (the one this file is in).

5. Click the puzzle-piece icon in the toolbar and pin Barnstorm, so its
   little plane is always there.

Chrome may ask now and then whether you want to keep extensions that
didn't come from its store. That's about how this was installed, not
about anything it's doing. Keeping it is fine.


PLAY

Go to any web page and click the plane (or press Alt+Shift+B).

  Click the plane again    pause, and get the page back; once more to resume
  P                        pause
  Esc, or the Land button  stop, and put the page back

The bar along the bottom of the window has the scores, a switch between
the two games, and the keys for the one you're in.

  WRECK    Up is the throttle, Left and Right turn, Down brakes, Space fires.
           Fly through crates for power-ups. Shoot the builders too.

  SORTIE   Arrows fly, Space fires straight ahead, X does a loop (you only
           get a few). One hit costs a plane, unless you have wingmen.
           Shoot the slow yellow one for a crate.


GOOD TO KNOW

- It only does anything on a page when you click its button, and only on
  that page. It doesn't watch what you browse, and it sends nothing
  anywhere. Your best scores are kept in the browser.
- It can't run on the browser's own pages (settings, the extension store,
  a PDF). The button says "no" for a moment if you try.
- Leave or reload the page and the game is gone. Pause is for when you
  want to come back to it.


REMOVE IT

Go back to chrome://extensions and click Remove on Barnstorm. Then you can
delete this folder.
