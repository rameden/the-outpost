Finance found customers on monthly plans with no February invoice. All of them were billed at the end of January.

## What happens

For a plan billed on 2026-01-31, the next billing date comes out as 2026-03-03. February gets no invoice at all, and the customer's billing day moves from the end of the month to the 3rd.

## How to reproduce

1. Take a monthly plan last billed on 2026-01-31.
2. Ask for its next billing date.

It gives 2026-03-03. A plan billed on 2026-03-31 gives 2026-05-01 instead of a date in April. Plans billed on the 28th or earlier are fine.

## What should happen

When the next month is too short to have the same day, bill on that month's last day: 2026-01-31 is followed by 2026-02-28, and 2026-03-31 by 2026-04-30. In a leap year, 2028-01-31 is followed by 2028-02-29.

Getting back to the 31st in the months after a short one needs the plan to remember its original billing day. That is a separate piece of work and not part of this ticket.

## Acceptance criteria

- The next billing date is always in the following calendar month
- If that month has no such day, it is the month's last day
- Leap years are handled: January 31, 2028 is followed by February 29, 2028
- Plans billed on the 1st to the 28th get the same dates as today
- The tests for this ticket pass and are no longer marked as open
