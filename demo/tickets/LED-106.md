This came out of the readiness review for our SOC 2 Type I audit and ISO 27001 certification. The reviewer asked who voided INV-0188 and when. The audit trail could not say.

## What happens

Sending an invoice and marking it paid each add an entry to the audit trail: who did it, to which invoice, and when. Voiding an invoice changes its status and adds nothing. The trail for a voided invoice ends at "sent".

## How to reproduce

1. Send an invoice, then void it as an admin, giving a reason.
2. Read the trail that comes back.

It has one entry, for the send. There is none for the void, and the reason that was given is kept nowhere.

## What should happen

Voiding adds one entry to the trail, like every other step: the time, the person, the action ("voided") and the invoice number. Its note is the reason that was given.

## Controls this is evidence for

- SOC 2, CC7.2: activity is recorded so that anything out of the ordinary can be found and looked into
- ISO/IEC 27001:2022, Annex A 8.15 (logging)

## Acceptance criteria

- Voiding an invoice adds exactly one entry to the trail
- The entry has the time, who did it, the action "voided" and the invoice number
- The entry's note is the reason given for the void
- Sending and paying add the same entries they add today
- The tests for this ticket pass and are no longer marked as open
