The STUDIO15 offer reads "15% off orders of $500 or more". A customer ordered exactly $500.00 of work, entered the code, and was told they hadn't spent enough.

## What happens

With a subtotal of exactly $500.00, STUDIO15 takes nothing off and the reason given is that the order is under the minimum spend. At $500.01 it works.

## How to reproduce

1. Make an invoice whose lines add up to exactly $500.00.
2. Apply the code STUDIO15.

No discount is applied.

## What should happen

$75.00 comes off (15% of $500.00). "Or more" includes the minimum itself. The same goes for any code with a minimum spend: LOYAL25 ($25 off orders of $100 or more) should work at exactly $100.00.

## Acceptance criteria

- A subtotal equal to a code's minimum spend gets the discount
- A subtotal one cent under the minimum still doesn't
- Codes with no minimum spend behave as they do now
- The tests for this ticket pass and are no longer marked as open
