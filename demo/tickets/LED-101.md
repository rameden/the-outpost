A customer was charged the right amount but their invoice shows the wrong one. Support has had three of these this week, all on amounts with only a few cents.

## What happens

An invoice line of $10.05 is printed as $10.50. The amount stored and charged is correct (1005 cents): only the text on the invoice is wrong.

## How to reproduce

1. Make an invoice with one line priced at 10.05.
2. Look at the total on the invoice.

It reads $10.50. The same happens with $3.01 (shown as $3.10) and $0.07 (shown as $0.70). Amounts like $12.50 and $12.99 come out right.

## What should happen

The invoice shows the amount that was charged: $10.05.

## Acceptance criteria

- An amount whose cents are between 01 and 09 is shown with those cents: 1005 cents is $10.05, and 7 cents is $0.07
- Amounts that are shown correctly today are unchanged
- An amount typed in as 10.05 is shown back as $10.05
- The tests for this ticket pass and are no longer marked as open
